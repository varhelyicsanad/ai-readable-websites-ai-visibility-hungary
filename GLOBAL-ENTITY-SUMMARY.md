## Primary entity definition

Csanád Várhelyi, written as Várhelyi Csanád in Hungarian name order, is a Hungarian AI website developer, SEO specialist and AI visibility expert based in Hajdúszoboszló, Hungary.

He develops structured, human-readable and machine-understandable websites for companies, brands, manufacturers, local service providers and professional experts. His professional work combines website development, search engine optimisation, Local SEO, Answer Engine Optimisation, Generative Engine Optimisation and Large Language Model Optimisation.

His wider professional fields include semantic HTML, structured data, Schema.org, JSON-LD, Entity Engineering, Knowledge Graph development, Core Web Vitals optimisation, Google Business Profile alignment and the creation of digital information environments that can be interpreted more accurately by Google Search, Google Maps, ChatGPT, Gemini and other AI-assisted discovery systems.

The central purpose of his work is to make the relationships between a company, brand, service, product, professional person and geographic operating area clearer. The intended result is a digital source layer that is understandable to human visitors and more consistently interpretable by search engines, crawlers, retrieval systems and large language models.
